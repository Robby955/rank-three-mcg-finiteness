# Review guide: curve maps and structure-sheaf cohomology

This update proves finiteness of actual H1(X,O_X) for specified classes of
proper curves. It supplies supporting mathematics for the normalizer
project. It does not prove the complete geometric section bound or a new
mapping-class-finite representation theorem.

## Start with the exported conclusions

Throughout this table k is a field. Every cohomology statement uses the
actual structure sheaf and the scalar action induced by the original
structure morphism p:X -> Spec k.

| Declaration | Geometric inputs | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| `finiteMap_projectiveLine_unit_H1_finite` in [FiniteMapH1](Normalizer/FiniteMapH1.lean) | Actual finite f:X -> P1_k | Actual H1(X,O_X) is finite-dimensional; no integrality, normality or reducedness input. |
| `properNormalCurve_exists_finite_projectiveLine` and `properNormalCurve_unit_H1_finite` in [CurveMapExistence](Normalizer/CurveMapExistence.lean) | Proper p, integral X, dimension at most one, integrally closed actual stalks | An actual finite map over p is constructed, giving actual H1 finiteness. |
| `properSmoothCurve_exists_finite_projectiveLine` and `properSmoothCurve_unit_H1_finite` in [CurveMapExistence](Normalizer/CurveMapExistence.lean) | Proper smooth p, integral X, dimension at most one | Same conclusions; actual stalk normality is derived. |
| `properCurve_exists_finite_projectiveLine_of_normalAwayPoint` and `properCurve_unit_H1_finite_of_normalAwayPoint` in [CurveMapSingular](Normalizer/CurveMapSingular.lean) | Proper p, integral X, dimension at most one; integrally closed stalks away from one specified point | Same conclusions; no normality at the exceptional point is assumed. |
| `proper_dimZero_exists_finite_projectiveLine` and `proper_dimZero_positiveCohomology_subsingleton` in [CurveMapExistence](Normalizer/CurveMapExistence.lean) | Proper p, dimension at most zero | Finite map exists, and positive-degree cohomology vanishes for every abelian sheaf; nilpotents are retained. |

The normal and smooth conclusions do not take a rational function,
nonconstancy certificate, finite map or H1 dimension bound as a premise.
The one-exceptional-point theorem does not assume the exceptional point has
an affine neighborhood containing every nonnormal point; it constructs one.

## Dependency order

1. [AffineH1Vanishing](Normalizer/AffineH1Vanishing.lean) and
   [SchemeOpenCohomology](Normalizer/SchemeOpenCohomology.lean) prove actual
   affine H1 vanishing and transport it to geometric affine opens.
2. [TwoAffineCohomology](Normalizer/TwoAffineCohomology.lean) and
   [TwoAffineSections](Normalizer/TwoAffineSections.lean) construct the
   actual Mayer-Vietoris quotient with the original field action.
3. [ProjectiveCoordinates](Normalizer/ProjectiveCoordinates.lean) identifies
   the actual Proj chart rings with polynomial rings and their overlap with
   Laurent polynomials. Both actual restriction formulas are proved.
4. [LaurentCechFinite](Normalizer/LaurentCechFinite.lean),
   [FiniteChartGeometry](Normalizer/FiniteChartGeometry.lean),
   [FiniteMapLaurent](Normalizer/FiniteMapLaurent.lean) and
   [FiniteMapH1](Normalizer/FiniteMapH1.lean) connect finite generators of
   actual pullback charts to the finite Laurent quotient. Localization,
   restriction images and coefficient compatibility are constructed.
5. [CurveRationalExtension](Normalizer/CurveRationalExtension.lean),
   [CurveMapExistence](Normalizer/CurveMapExistence.lean) and
   [CurveMapSingular](Normalizer/CurveMapSingular.lean) construct the needed
   nonconstant map locally and extend it. Agreement on the entire original
   domain preserves nonconstancy. Properness then proves finiteness.
6. The parallel section-sequence chain, ending in
   [SectionCohomologyDimension](Normalizer/SectionCohomologyDimension.lean),
   supplies actual restriction/cokernel comparisons, linear exactness,
   finite kernels and the line-section H1 finiteness equivalence.
   These results support the later degree argument; they do not define a
   line's degree to be the length of the constructed zero scheme.

[CORRESPONDENCE.md](CORRESPONDENCE.md) links every audited declaration to
its exact source line and describes its mathematical role. Private helper
proofs are checked by compilation; the named-declaration count does not
count every internal declaration generated by Lean.

## Remaining obligations

For a general proper integral curve with at least two points, construct a
nontrivial affine open containing the whole nonnormal locus. The current
`properCurve_exists_finite_projectiveLine_of_normalOutsideAffine` proves the
finite-map conclusion given that actual open and normality of its complement.
It does not construct that input for arbitrary singular curves. Finite
normalization with its defect and cohomology transfer is an alternative
unformalized route. The general target has not acquired a normality assumption.

Higher vanishing on curves, Euler/degree comparison and the specified
nonzero determinant section's nowhere-vanishing implication remain.
The complete h0(E/M)<=4 bound, deriving its inputs from representations,
exhaustive genus-five coverage and upstream finiteness obligations remain
separate. The candidate endpoint and rank-three claims retain their labels.

```sh
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
python3 scripts/verify.py
python3 scripts/check_correspondence.py
git diff --exit-code -- AxiomAudit.lean lake-manifest.json
```
